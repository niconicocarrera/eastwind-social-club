ABOUT US

East Wind is a music venue and social club that highlights local talent, with an emphasis on Latino-Black Chicago and local music traditions.

East Wind is a love letter to immigrant communities, especially to people who don't always feel like they belong in traditional music spaces. Music gives people a way to express themselves fully, without asking for permission.
